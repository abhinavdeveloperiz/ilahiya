Academic Regulations

Attendance

  • Regular attendance is mandatory. Students must attend at least 75% of classes each semester.
  • Medical leave must be submitted with valid documentation.

Examination

The college follows a comprehensive examination system including university semester exams and internal assessments.

Semester Exams

The 5-year program has 10 semesters and the 3-year program has 6 semesters. Semester exams are conducted every 6 months by MG University.

Internal Assessment

Internal assessments include two exams, one presentation, and one assignment. The best internal exam score will be considered.

Academic Integrity

Plagiarism and dishonesty are not tolerated and will result in disciplinary action.

Campus Discipline

College Hours

9:30 AM to 3:30 PM. The Principal may make schedule adjustments when necessary.

Punctuality

Students must arrive on time for classes, exams, and official college events.

Uniform Dress Code

Boys: Black pants, white shirt, and college emblem/badge.

Girls: Black pants, white shirt, black blazer, and college emblem/badge.

Code of Conduct

Students are expected to maintain respect, discipline, and decorum across campus.

Other Regulations

Library Rules

Follow borrowing policies, return deadlines, and quiet study rules.

Moot Court

Participation is encouraged to improve legal research and advocacy skills.

Extracurricular Activities

Participate in sports, culture, and clubs to develop leadership and teamwork.

Anti-Ragging

Ragging is strictly prohibited and violators will face disciplinary action.

Sexual Harassment

Zero tolerance policy. Report any incidents to the relevant authorities.

General Guidelines

Communication

Students should regularly check college notices and announcements.

Feedback

Share feedback on courses, faculty, and facilities to improve campus life.

Grievance Redressal

Approach the committee with any concerns or issues for prompt resolution.

Fee Payment

Semester fees must be paid on time to remain eligible for promotion.

Final Authority

The Principal's decision is final for all matters related to rules and regulations.
