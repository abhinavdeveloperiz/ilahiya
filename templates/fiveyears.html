Eligibility & Admission Criteria

Meeting these requirements is essential for admission to our program

(i) Nativity Requirements

Citizenship & Documentation

  • • The applicant must be an Indian citizen.
  • • Only candidates of Kerala origin are eligible for reservation benefits or fee concessions.

Required documents to establish Indian citizenship (any one):

  • ✓ Birth certificate from Panchayat/Municipality/Corporation showing place of birth in India
  • ✓ SSLC certificate showing place of birth in any Indian state
  • ✓ Valid passport issued by Government of India
  • ✓ Certificate from Village Officer confirming Indian nativity

For Kerala origin candidates (any one):

  • ✓ SSLC certificate showing Kerala as place of birth
  • ✓ Birth certificate indicating Kerala as place of birth
  • ✓ Passport of candidate or parents showing Kerala as birthplace
  • ✓ Certificate from Village Officer/Tahsildar confirming Kerala nativity

(ii) Academic Qualifications

  • • Completed Higher Secondary Examination (HSE) or equivalent recognized by Kerala universities
  • • 10+2 schooling or equivalent qualifications recognized by Bar Council of India (BCI)
  • • Higher Secondary Pass Certificate through distance education or correspondence courses
  • • Students appearing for qualifying examination may apply (must produce final pass certificate at admission)

(iii) Age Criteria

  • • Must have completed 17 years of age as of December 31st of the admission year
  • • Upper age limit subject to final decision of Hon'ble Supreme Court of India
  • • Age restrictions under Clause 28 of Legal Education Rules, 2008 (BCI) may apply if reinstated

This comprehensive admission framework ensures that only eligible and qualified candidates gain entry to our Integrated Double Degree Course in Law, maintaining high academic standards and fostering competent legal professionals.
