Admission Procedure

Admission Procedure for Ilahia Law College

This page covers the full admission process, eligibility rules, application details and seat allotment guidelines.

Eligibility for Admission

A candidate who has passed the Plus Two ( +2 ) examination under the Government of Kerala or an equivalent board exam recognized by Mahatma Gandhi University with at least 45% for general candidates and 40% for SC/ST candidates is eligible for our law programs.

  • Kerala Law Entrance Examination is required for Government quota admission to 3-year LLB and 5-year BBA.LLB courses.
  • Applications are invited for entrance exams to government and private law colleges participating in the seat sharing agreement.

Eligibility Criteria for 5-Year Courses

The five-year integrated LLB program requires strong academic performance, minimum marks and age criteria. These rules ensure candidates are prepared for advanced legal education.

  • Passed HSC or equivalent exam with at least 45% aggregate marks (42% for SEBC, 40% for SC/ST).
  • Minimum age is 17 years.
  • No upper age limit applies.
  • Applicants must be Indian citizens.

Eligibility for LLB Course

Admission to the 3-year LLB course is open to graduates from any recognized university, subject to minimum marks and documentation requirements.

  • Applicant should be an Indian citizen.
  • Graduation with at least 45% aggregate marks (40% for SC/ST candidates).
  • Provisional or degree certificate must be produced at admission.

Qualifying Standards in the Entrance Exam

  • General / SEBC candidates must secure at least 10% marks in the entrance exam.
  • SC/ST candidates must secure at least 5% marks.
  • The exam is a 3-hour test of General English, General Knowledge and Aptitude for Legal Studies.

How and When to Apply

Apply online through www.cee.kerala.gov.in. Upload a recent passport-size colour photograph in JPG format, with size 15-30 KB and dimensions 150x200 pixels.

Application Fee

  • General / SEBC candidates pay Rs. 685; SC/ST candidates pay Rs. 345.
  • Fee can be remitted at State Bank of Travancore branches using the challan.

Application Submission

Print the online application, attach a passport-size photograph, and have it attested by a Gazette Officer. Send the documents to the Commissioner for Entrance Examinations, Housing Board Buildings, 5th Floor, Santhi Nagar, Thiruvananthapuram-695001 by registered post, speed post or hand delivery.

Seat Allotment

  • Admission allotments are based on rank, institution preferences and the Centralized Allotment Process.
  • CAP details are published on official websites and in newspapers; individual intimation is not sent.
  • All allotments follow the rules in the official prospectus for the Three Year LL.B Course, Kerala.
