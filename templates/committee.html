Library Committee

Supporting knowledge and research

The Library Committee shapes library planning, policy and user services so every student and faculty member can access the resources they need for learning, research and campus success.

Objectives

  • Recommend books and digital resources
  • Oversee circulation, reference and study support
  • Respond to user needs and library feedback

Importance

  • Ensures the library supports academic goals
  • Improves access to learning materials
  • Strengthens research support campus-wide

Legal Aid Clinic

Justice through service

The Legal Aid Clinic delivers free legal support to underserved communities while offering students practical experience in advocacy, counseling, and client engagement.

Key goals

  • Provide free or low-cost legal services
  • Promote access to justice for all
  • Empower communities with legal rights awareness

Campus impact

  • Strengthens social responsibility
  • Builds practical training for law students
  • Supports equal access to legal remedies

SC/ST/OBC Welfare Committee

Promoting inclusivity and fair grievance support

This committee ensures equal opportunity, addresses complaints sensitively, and supports students from SC/ST/OBC backgrounds with counseling and guidance.

Objectives

  • Foster diversity and equal opportunity
  • Provide a safe grievance platform
  • Offer support for challenged students

Functions

  • Investigate grievances fairly
  • Deliver counseling and guidance
  • Organize inclusivity awareness programs

Internal Complaints Committee

Keeping the campus safe and respectful

The ICC receives complaints of harassment, conducts confidential inquiries, and ensures that every student can learn in a secure environment.

Responsibilities

  • Receive and investigate complaints
  • Protect confidentiality and dignity
  • Recommend actions in line with policy

Impact

  • Supports gender equality and a safe campus
  • Promotes awareness and prevention
  • Builds trust through fair redressal

Internal Marks Grievance Cell

Fair evaluation for every student

The Internal Marks Grievance Cell resolves concerns about assessments, exam conduct and grading so evaluation remains transparent and students feel supported.

Core aims

  • Ensure fairness in grading
  • Provide a clear grievance pathway
  • Support students through review processes

Student support

  • Investigate mark-related complaints
  • Maintain transparency in evaluation
  • Collect and act on student feedback

Disciplinary Committee

Maintaining campus discipline with fairness

This committee investigates misconduct, resolves conflicts, and ensures that campus discipline is upheld fairly and transparently.

Objectives

  • Uphold institutional rules and standards
  • Resolve conflicts respectfully
  • Protect students and staff equally

Functions

  • Investigate rule violations
  • Recommend appropriate actions
  • Maintain a fair grievance process
