Academic Resources

Library Rules & Regulations

Students are requested to maintain proper discipline and follow the library guidelines to ensure a peaceful and productive academic environment at Ilahia Law College.

Maintain Silence

Complete silence must be maintained inside the library premises to provide a calm and focused study atmosphere.

Carry ID Card

Students must carry their valid college ID card while entering and using library facilities.

No Food Allowed

Eating, drinking and carrying food items inside the library are strictly prohibited.

Handle Books Carefully

Library books and resources should be handled responsibly. Damaging or marking books is strictly prohibited.

Keep Phones Silent

Mobile phones should be kept in silent mode while inside the library to avoid disturbance to others.

Return Books On Time

Borrowed books must be returned on or before the due date. Late returns may attract penalties as per library policy.

Compliance & Student Welfare

Ilahia Law College is committed to maintaining a safe, respectful and inclusive academic environment through anti-ragging initiatives, counselling support, statutory committees and grievance redressal mechanisms.

Anti-Ragging Cell

Ragging in any form is strictly prohibited within the campus. The institution follows UGC Anti-Ragging Regulations to ensure a safe learning environment.
